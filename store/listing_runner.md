**Title:** Catch Zibo! Snow Runner
**Short:** Chase the goblin who stole the village gold! Run, jump, slide & catch him!
**Full:**
Zibo the goblin stole all the gold of the snowy village. Grab your snowballs and chase him!

• Swipe to switch lanes, jump over gifts and slide under banners
• Tap to throw snowballs at Zibo and catch him for big bonuses
• Run through 4 worlds: the Christmas village, pine forest, frozen lake and Zibo's spooky castle
• Unlock 16 heroes. Each one has a special power
• Collect power-ups: magnet, double gold, super jump and Santa's flying sleigh

• Beat your best score and share it with friends
